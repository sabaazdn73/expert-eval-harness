A 9 m by 9 m floor bay has girders on its two sides at x = 0 and x = 9 m. Secondary beams span 9 m between the girders at y = 3 m and y = 6 m, and beams on the column lines at y = 0 and y = 9 m. The one-way slab spans 3 m between the beams. Dead loads: slab and finishes 5.5 kN/m², beam self-weight 3.375 kN/m (same section for all beams and headers).

A 3 m by 3 m stair opening at x = 3 to 6 m, y = 1.5 to 4.5 m cuts the beam at y = 3 m. The cut beam becomes two 3 m tail beams (x = 0 to 3 m and x = 6 to 9 m) that land on headers at x = 3 m and x = 6 m. Each header spans 6 m from the beam at y = 0 to the beam at y = 6 m and carries only its self-weight and the tail beam reaction. The slab strips next to the opening cantilever from the beams at y = 0 and y = 6 m, so those two beams keep their usual slab load.

Please find the dead-load reaction of a tail beam, the dead-load reaction of a header, and the midspan dead-load moment of the beam at y = 6 m. All members are simply supported.
