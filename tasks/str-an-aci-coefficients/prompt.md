A girder is continuous over four equal spans of 9 m between the centrelines of 500 × 500 mm columns, built integrally with the columns. It is prismatic and carries uniform loads: dead load 24.585 kN/m and live load 7.20 kN/m (unfactored); the factored load is 1.2D + 1.6L = 41.02 kN/m.

Please check whether the simplified method of ACI 318-19 Section 6.5 may be used, and if so find the factored moments at the interior face of the exterior support, positive in the end span, and at the exterior face of the first interior support.
