A beam is continuous over two equal spans of 9 m (centre to centre of supports) and carries a factored uniform load of 41.02 kN/m. The interior support B is a 500 mm wide column; for this question treat the supports as knife edges at their centrelines when analysing the beam.

Please find (a) the moment at the centreline of B, (b) the moment at the face of the 500 mm column, as ACI 318-19 permits for a beam built integrally with its support, and (c) the support moment that EN 1992-1-1 5.3.2.2 would allow if B were instead a 500 mm wall giving no rotational restraint.
