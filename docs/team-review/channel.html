**FINDINGS (seismic-demand)**

Sign convention: +x east, +y north. Relative movement = garage minus office. The bridge closes or opens the joint by the same magnitude, so I report absolute envelope values. Clause numbers are from memory and are unverified, because no ASCE 7-22 text is in the package.

1. **Location of the garage displacement: TSF-2611 is wrong.**
   - TSF uses δe = 0.12 in. at joint 100, the center of mass. The bearing is at x = 126 ft, y = 256.5 ft.
   - Table 40.6, EX, Ux varies linearly with y: 0.040 at y = 0 and 0.200 at y = 270. At y = 256.5 this gives **0.192 in.**
   - The Rz route gives 0.192 in. as well (Rz = 4.95E-5 rad × 3,240 in. = 0.160 in., so the two agree).
   - The 2-ft offset east of grid G changes Uy by only about 0.001 in. and does not change Ux.
   - Using joint 100 understates the garage demand by about 38%.
   - N-S: EY Uy = 0.133 at joint 77, and 0.134 in. at x = 126 ft.
   - Cross-check: δmax/δavg = 0.200/0.120 = 1.67 and Ax = (1.67/1.2)² = 1.93, which matches the calc.
   - **Draft comment 2 is wrong.** 0.12 in. matches the joint 100 row, but that is not the bearing location.

2. **Amplification: TSF is wrong for the office building.**
   - TSF amplifies the garage by Cd = 5 but uses K&M's 0.32 in. unamplified. K&M states in item 5 that the values are not amplified.
   - K&M values ×Cd/Ie (5.5/1.0): E-W 0.32 → **1.76 in.**; N-S 0.29 → **1.60 in.**
   - This is the largest error. The office contribution is understated by a factor of 5.5.
   - K&M's 0.32 and 0.29 in. match their enclosure (Ux 0.320 and Uy 0.290). Their period basis (T = 1.12 s per 12.8.6.2) is acceptable for displacements.
   - I can't verify their model, and I can't check the building height or T.
   - MCE factor: I recall 12.12.3 for separation and 12.12.4 for spanning members, and I don't know whether 7-22 requires a 1.5 factor. S-001 note 7 cites 12.12.4. Verify against a licensed copy. I show the ×1.5 result as a sensitivity only.

3. **Diaphragm deformation: add it, conservatively.**
   - §40.5 states that δe excludes δdi. Table 40.6A, L2 X: 0 at y = 0, 0.051 at y = 135, 0.124 at y = 270. At y = 256.5 this interpolates to **0.117 in.** (a quadratic fit gives 0.116). Y: 0.039 in.
   - I recall 12.8.6.3 as the diaphragm-deflection clause (verify). Because the bridge sits at the slab edge, δdi is a real relative movement at the bearing.
   - Caveat: δdi is computed under Fpx = 928 k, versus the ELF story force of 383 k at L2. Scaling to ELF forces would give about 0.05 in. The Fpx value is conservative.
   - I added δdi to δe before applying Cd.

4. **Governing results (E-W, 100% direction, no thermal).**

| Case | Garage | Office | SRSS | Absolute sum |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| TSF as submitted | 0.60 | 0.32 | 0.68 | — |
| Corrected, no δdi | 0.96 | 1.76 | 2.01 | 2.72 |
| **Corrected with δdi (governing)** | **1.54** | **1.76** | **2.34** | **3.30** |
| ×1.5 MCE sensitivity (unverified) | — | — | — | 4.96 |

   - Governing E-W movement is 3.30 in. absolute (2.34 in. SRSS), against TSF's 0.68 in. The absolute sum is 3.2 times TSF's total of 1.04 in. once thermal is included.
   - **N-S:** garage 5 × 0.134 = 0.67 in. (0.86 in. with δdi); office 1.60 in. SRSS is 1.73 to 1.81 in. and the absolute sum is 2.27 to 2.46 in. TSF only checked N-S against travel, with no stated demand.
   - Orthogonal 100%+30% (12.5): E-W absolute 3.46 in., N-S 2.59 in. The effect is small.

5. **Combination: SRSS is not appropriate for the bearing.**
   - The separation provision allows SRSS because the two buildings' peaks are not simultaneous.
   - For a bearing, the office end is fixed and the garage end slides. I recommend using the absolute sum for travel and seat. SRSS can be accepted only if the licensed 7-22 clause confirms it applies to spanning members.
   - Draft comment 3 is not supportable as written.

6. **Draft comments 1 and 4, from my side.**
   - Travel of 5.0 in. and the 7 in. joint still exceed 3.30 + 0.36 = 3.66 in. (E-W, absolute sum, with full thermal). Nominal capacity is not exceeded.
   - Two things limit that: the ×1.5 sensitivity of 4.96 + 0.36 = 5.3 in. would exceed 5.0 in., and the pad is a rigid 6 in. element. The "OK" verdicts rest on the wrong demand.
   - The review should not state that travel is adequate until TSF resubmits.

7. **Context: torsional irregularity and analysis procedure.**
   - TIR = 1.67 > 1.4, so this is Type 1b (extreme), not just "Type 1." The calc and S-001 should say 1b.
   - As I recall, 12.3.3.1 prohibits extreme torsion only in SDC E/F, so SDC D is permitted. Ax is capped at 3.0 and the 1.93 is fine.
   - **Possible larger issue, from memory (verify Table 12.6-1):** for SDC D, a structure with a Type 1a/1b irregularity above two stories above the base may not be permitted to use ELF (modal or response-history analysis required). The garage has three levels above L1 and uses ELF. If this is confirmed, δe and Ax must be recomputed. That would affect the whole of Section 40, not only SB-7.

8. **What the submittal must state.** S-001 deferred note 3 requires the submittal to list the displacements imposed on the garage. TSF gives only one scalar and no per-direction values.

**Verdict:** TSF §5 is not acceptable. The draft's "NO EXCEPTIONS TAKEN" is not supportable on the seismic demand.

**FINDINGS**

**A. Imposed loads on the garage (S-001 deferred note 3, S-401 note 5)**

1. **Vertical reaction. Arithmetic OK, but incomplete.**
   - The fabricator's numbers check: D = 1.22×66/2 = 40.3 k, and L = 90 psf×12 ft×66/2 = 35.6 k. 90 psf matches S-001.
   - The W24x84 end diaphragm is omitted. It adds about 0.84 k (84 plf×10 ft), so D ≈ 41.1 k. This is small, but the calc should include it.
   - Factored (ASCE 7-22 §2.3.1): 1.2D+1.6L = 105 k. With 41.1 k it is 106 k. Vertical seismic Ev = 0.2·SDS·D ≈ 8 k.
   - TSF-2611 gives only service P = 76 k. It states no factored reaction, no seismic vertical, and no combinations.
2. **Horizontal force. Not stated. Required.**
   - S-001 deferred note 3 requires the loads and displacements imposed on the garage. TSF-2611 gives none for horizontal force, so it fails the note.
   - If the PTFE slides, the friction force is μN. At an assumed μ of 0.06–0.10 (my assumption, not in BDS-614) on 76 k, that is 4.6–7.6 k. If the PTFE sticks, the elastomer shears instead, and I can't size that without BDS-614.
   - ACI 318-19 §16.5 (the Nuc minimum, with clause numbers to be verified) sets a corbel floor of 0.2×Vu ≈ 21 k unless provisions prevent tensile force. This is probably what governs the corbel, not friction.
   - The submittal must state the design horizontal force. Only the EOR can say whether C-1 was designed for the greater of 0.2Vu and the friction force.
3. **Eccentricity and SB-1 torsion.**
   - The pad centre is 12 in. from the SB-1 face at neutral, and the sheet runs 4 in. to 20 in. The pad edge reaches 20 in. at +5 in. of travel, and the corbel ends at 24 in.
   - At +5 in. the load arm is 17 in. from the face. The torsion arm about the SB-1 centreline is about 24 in. at neutral and 29 in. at +5 in.
   - Tu ≈ 106 k × 2.0 ft ≈ 211 k-ft at neutral, and about 255 k-ft at +5 in. Under my assumptions, the horizontal-force effect adds to this.
   - **The corbel and SB-1 design calcs are not in the package** (S-401 notes 8 and 13, and 4/S-501). I can't confirm that 5-#7 top bars, A706 Gr 60, f'c = 5,000 psi and a 3'-0" width cover these loads at any pad position. The EOR must confirm the design basis.
   - I also can't tell from S-401 where the inside face of the C2 anchor bar sits. The bearing area must not project past it (ACI §16.5 detailing). Please confirm this at +5 in. of travel.

**B. Draft review comments**

- **Comment 1 (travel exceeds movement, no stops): reject as written.** TSF-2611 uses 0.60 in. for the garage, which is Cd×δe at joint 100, the centre of mass. The bearing is at x = 126 ft, y = 256.5 ft.

  - The office end is wrong too. K&M's 0.32 in. is explicitly unamplified. With Cd = 5.5 and Ie = 1.0, it becomes **1.76 in.** TSF-2611 used 0.32 in.
  - Design-level movement at the bearing:

    | Combination | Without thermal | With 0.36 in. thermal |
    |---|---|---|
    | SRSS | 2.00 in. | 2.36 in. |
    | Absolute sum | 2.72 in. | 3.08 in. |

  - δdi at L2 is about 0.116 in. at y = 256.5, from a quadratic fit through Table 40.6A, or 0.118 in. linear. It adds to these figures. Whether it is also amplified by Cd is unresolved. If it is, it adds about 0.58 in.
  - If a 1.5× MCE factor applies (unverified for ASCE 7-22), the totals become 3.37 in. SRSS or 4.44 in. absolute with thermal. Adding δdi gives about 3.5–4.6 in. (5.0 in. with Cd on δdi).
  - Against 5.0 in. of travel, the margin is little to none at MCE level. The fabricator's ratio of 0.21 is wrong.
  - Note 3 on S-401 is the EOR's own "no keeper bars or end stops" note. It needs revisiting, because a pad leaving the sheet means lost support.
