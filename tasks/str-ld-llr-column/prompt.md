A four-storey office building has a 9 m by 9 m column grid. Consider an interior column in the ground storey. Above it are three office floors (levels 2, 3 and 4) and the roof. Each floor gives the column a tributary area of 81 m².

The office live load is 2.40 kN/m² (50 psf) and the floors also carry the 0.72 kN/m² (15 psf) partition allowance. Using ASCE 7-22, please find the floor live load on this column from the three office floors (do not include the roof), reduced where the standard permits. Give the reduction factor you used, the office live load, the partition load and the total.
