A 400 × 600 mm reinforced concrete girder spans 9 m between the centrelines of two 500 × 500 mm columns and carries a factored uniform load of 41.02 kN/m. At each end there is a column below and a column above, each 3.75 m long, with their far ends built in. All members have the same modulus of elasticity.

Following ACI 318-19 for a factored-load analysis (member stiffness from Table 6.6.3.1.1(a); far ends of the columns fixed as permitted by 6.3.1.2), please find the girder moment at each end, at the joint (on the column centreline, not at the column face), and at midspan.
